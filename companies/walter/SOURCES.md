## Left out on purpose
- **Revenue potential.** The Commitment Days deck shows €153k p.a. (slide 36) and €144k p.a. (appendix), pipeline >€177k and >€240k. Inconsistent and hypothetical, so left out.
- **Pilot count.** Older sources say 15 or 18; the film uses 22 from the final Commitment Days deck.
- **Time savings.** One property manager reported a third less communication time (Granola, 15 Oct), the ads claim 40 %.
  The IZ draft quotes Christoph: "Wir haben noch keine festen Zahlen." No savings figure in the film.
- **Customer and lead names** (pilot customers, the large investor, retail chains): no written OK.
- **Money:** budget, funding round, cap table, founder package. Deal terms and internal.
- **Launch event and dates.** Timeless rule.
- **Interview.** Fred decided: no interview footage. The film is voice-over only, so no person is named as founder or managing director.
- **Commercial today.** The product is residential first (Demo prep 21 Oct: commercial not covered yet), so commercial appears only as a test phase.
