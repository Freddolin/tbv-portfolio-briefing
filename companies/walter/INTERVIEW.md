# walter: interview to record

No interview footage exists yet. The film needs four short answers (f1 to f4). Record in English.

**On camera (proposal):** Christoph Wend-Erdel for f1, f3, f4. For f2, either Christoph or a Drees & Sommer partner
(Karsten Eisenmann or Thomas Häusser). A partner's voice makes "what it means for Drees & Sommer" more credible.
If a partner answers f2, add them to `founders` in content.yaml, set `founder: 1` on f2 and end the q_ds line with their first name.

| Segment | Question | Length | What a good answer contains |
|---|---|---|---|
| f1 | What is walter? Give one example from a property manager's day. | 15–20 s | One concrete case (a tenant message, an invoice), what walter does, that the manager approves |
| f2 | What does walter mean for Drees & Sommer? | 15–20 s | The idea came from D&S consulting; closer to buildings in operation; what D&S teams can bring |
| f3 | What does the walter team get from The Bau Ventures? | 15–20 s | Speed (Build First, AI prototypes), the D&S network and name, backing to build a company |
| f4 | The Bau Ventures, in three words? | 5–8 s | Three words, short pause between them |

**Avoid in the answers:** numbers (customers, savings, money), customer names, dates and events, the word "founder".

**Recording:** landscape, at least 1080p, 25 fps; quiet room; clip-on mic or a separate recorder (clap once for sync);
head and shoulders with some room above the head (the template crops a portrait card from the first frame);
two takes per question. Drop the files into companies/walter/media/ (interview.mp4, plus audio if separate).
