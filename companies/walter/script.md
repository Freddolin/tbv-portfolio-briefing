## problem
Property management runs on manual work. [pause] Tenant emails, phone calls, invoices, service charge and utility bill statements, owner meetings, each in a different tool. [pause] Good staff are hard to find, and margins are under pressure. [pause] So every new building means more work for the same people.

## handover
This is where Valter comes in: [pause] an A I colleague for property managers, from Berlin, [pause] and the first venture from The Bau Ventures' studio to launch fully on the market.

## how
Valter is not another tool. He works like a new colleague. [pause] Every building gets its own inbox. He reads each request, opens a ticket and books the contractor. [pause] He also handles documents and deadlines, invoices and banking, utility billing, owner meetings, letting and handovers, and an app for tenants and owners. [pause] Valter prepares the work. The property manager approves. [pause] And this is only the first version. [pause] Valter keeps growing along its roadmap, towards the whole property in one system: property, asset and facility management, up to the sale.

## team
Valter was built in-house, and it started with a problem. [pause] Drees and Sommer partner Thomas Häusser and associate partner Karsten Eisenmann saw property managers under growing margin pressure, needing to make their processes far more efficient. [pause] They brought that problem to us. [pause] In our Build First process, Christoph Wend-Erdel, Alexander Assimidis and the team quickly built first prototypes, tested them with property managers and validated their assumptions. [pause] Customer feedback shaped the product that is now on the market. [pause] Christoph and Alexander lead the company as managing directors, together with Philipp Piepenstock, Valentin Heidrich and Paul Koch.

## traction
And the market is responding. [pause] After only the first marketing tests, more than two hundred and eighty leads are already in the sales funnel, and more than one hundred and seventy demos are done. [pause] Thirty customers are already live on Valter, managing over three thousand two hundred units. [pause] The first customers are now being converted to paying contracts. [pause] Next in line: a pilot with seven thousand seven hundred units, and more commercial pilot customers.

## next
The opportunity is large. [pause] Software for property management in Germany, Austria and Switzerland is a market of around one and a half billion euros a year. [pause] Valter starts with mid-size residential property managers, runs alongside the software they already use, and is priced per unit, so it grows with every portfolio. [pause] What comes next? [pause] Turn the pilots into paying customers. [pause] Grow from residential into commercial property management. [pause] Prepare the first funding round with external investors. [pause] And complete the team, including co-founders.

## ds_d
How can Drees and Sommer help? [pause] Thomas Häusser and Karsten Eisenmann remain the business unit partners behind Valter. [pause] Their network has already opened doors to large retail and real estate companies. [pause] What helps most now: introductions to asset managers, portfolio holders and property managers who are open to a pilot. [pause] In parallel, the team is in exchange with Drees and Sommer on its own utility billing review.

## close
Valter. [pause] The A I colleague for property management.
