## problem
Property management runs on manual work. [pause] Tenant emails, phone calls, invoices, service charge and utility bill statements, owner meetings, each in a different tool. [pause] And good staff are hard to find. [pause] So every new building means more work for the same people.

## handover
Valter, from Berlin, is an A I colleague for property managers, [pause] and the first venture from The Bau Ventures' studio to launch fully on the market.

## how
Valter is not another tool. He works like a new colleague. [pause] Every building gets its own inbox. Valter reads each request, opens a ticket and books the contractor. [pause] He also handles documents and deadlines, invoices and banking, utility billing, owner meetings, letting and handovers, and an app for tenants and owners. [pause] Valter prepares the work. The property manager approves. [pause] Next comes the whole property in one system: property, asset and facility management, up to the sale.

## team
Valter was built in-house. [pause] The idea came from Drees and Sommer partner Thomas Häusser and associate partner Karsten Eisenmann. [pause] The studio team built it with A I, from first prototype to market launch within months. [pause] Christoph Wend-Erdel and Alexander Assimidis lead the company as managing directors, together with Philipp Piepenstock, Valentin Heidrich and Paul Koch.

## traction
And the market is responding. [pause] More than two hundred and eighty leads are in the sales funnel, and more than one hundred and seventy demos are done. [pause] Thirty customers are already live on Valter, managing over three thousand two hundred units, with a revenue potential of over one hundred and fifty thousand euros a year. [pause] The first customers are now being converted to paying contracts. [pause] Next in line: a pilot with seven thousand seven hundred units, and more commercial pilot customers.

## next
What comes next? [pause] Turn the pilots into paying customers. [pause] Grow from residential into commercial property management. [pause] Prepare the first funding round with external investors. [pause] And complete the team, including co-founders.

[pause] What helps most now: introductions to asset managers, portfolio holders and property managers who are open to a pilot.
