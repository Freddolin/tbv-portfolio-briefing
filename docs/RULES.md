# Content rules

These come from feedback on the first four briefings (Naeva, roam, mod, Optiml). `python -m briefing check`
flags the obvious cases; the rest is judgement.

## Always
- **Timeless.** No dates, months, quarters or upcoming events ("in November", "this quarter", "next week").
  Describe what is planned and what it is for instead ("A joint workshop maps the use cases. Then come the first pilots.").
- **Tangible.** Say what the product actually does and for whom. Name the concrete things it connects or produces.
- **Drees & Sommer value early and concrete.** The D&S part follows the fixed four screens:
  why it matters · who brings what · use cases by business unit / team · who leads and what comes next.
  Use cases name the D&S unit or project phase and the client trigger.
- **Sources.** Every fact in the script must come from a source (deal memo, meeting notes, website, the founder).
  If a number is inconsistent between sources, round it ("a dozen") or leave it out.
- **Internal use only** tag stays on.

## Never
- Deal terms: ticket, stake, valuation, round size, co-investors.
- Operational details that do not help a D&S colleague use the company (budget approvals, internal processes).
- Customer names or references without written OK; check NDAs (example: mod's retail customer).
- Revenue / ARR / customer names unless the founder approved them for this film.
- Statements that only one side of the partnership agreed to (e.g. calling something a "co-creation").

## Sign-off
- Fred approves script and on-screen texts before voices are generated.
- Everything about Drees & Sommer is signed off by the responsible D&S partner(s) (requested by Nico).

## Voice-over style
- Short sentences, spoken English, no jargon. Abbreviations as spoken: "A I", "H S E", "C O two".
- Company names the voice mispronounces get a phonetic spelling in `script.md` (Optiml → "Optimal").
  Names of people: check pronunciation in the take, use phonetic spelling if needed ("Yurgen").
- Use `[pause]` between thoughts. Each section ends with a full sentence.
- The questions before founder segments end with the founder's first name ("So what does that mean for Drees and Sommer? Luke.").
